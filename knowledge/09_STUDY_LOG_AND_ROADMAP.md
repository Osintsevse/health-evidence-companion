# Study log and development roadmap

English migration dated 2026-10-06. General project history only; private installation links and user-specific operational identifiers from the earlier working copy have been omitted.

## Work actually completed in the source edition

Fourteen thematic modules, an index, a general-knowledge policy, five blank template files and an 88-entry source register were created. Available clinical sections covered colds, medicine safety, reasoning, laboratory results and allergy diagnosis. WHO CDDR/mhGAP and medical AI descriptions were reviewed to the extent stated in the source register.

NHS dose examples and the Serbian Tantum Verde Forte 3 mg/mL PIL were examined as general examples. Complete textbooks, CDDR, mhGAP, GINA 2026, all Russian clinical guidelines and all Serbian guides were not read.

## Literature approach

MSD Professional chapters support focused clinical learning. WHO Good Prescribing (1994) and Teacher's Guide (2001) provide a historical method with current doses checked elsewhere. ICD-11 CDDR (2024), mhGAP (2023), NICE/local guidance, Cochrane and primary studies support specific questions.

Harrison, Davidson, Katzung and Stahl are lawful-access candidates; access/current editions are not established and they are not claimed as read or used to justify a personal regimen.

## Development rule

Version 0.2.0 adds four topic modules and forty source entries (128 total), including three clearly marked secondary discovery lists. Selected ARIA/EAACI/WAO, laboratory, WHO/Batut/Rospotrebnadzor, treatment-review and regulator sections were inspected. Source access was audited for all 88 original register URLs and forty other documentation links. Metadata access is distinct from reading a full document or verifying every clinical assertion. Module 18 separates current recommendations, uncertain effects and pending appraisal; no broad drug blacklist or new clinical qualification is claimed.

For a new general question, read the relevant section, create original notes, add sources/date and update related modules. A found link is not a studied topic. Recheck currency at each new decision; do not promise autonomous background updates.

## Earlier limited evaluation

Three original skills passed structural checks. Independent synthetic scenarios retained unknown cold-product composition, avoided an unsupported dose or universal venlafaxine withdrawal plan and separated sensitization from food allergy. A displayed best spirometry result was clarified as not proving a single attempt. This was limited workflow evaluation, not clinical certification or diagnostic accuracy measurement.

One private record skill was saved in the earlier environment. Installation attempts for the earlier primary-care and medication-review skills were rejected by a server safety scan, including a narrowed informational revision. The message did not give a detailed reason. Those attempts are not evidence of approval; structural checks do not imply platform safety approval. This package must undergo its own review without concealing its medical purpose.

## Pharmacology, interactions and AI expansion

Selected MSD pharmacokinetics/pharmacodynamics/bioavailability sections, NHS SPS interaction mechanisms/resources/formulations/QT, and FDA/Medsafe warnings were studied. Modules 11 and 12 were added. Complete Stockley's, BNF, Lexidrug and Katzung were not read.

MedGemma, BioMistral, MedRAG, Infermedica, Isabel, Ada, EvidenceMD, DrugBank and public information APIs were compared by documentation. Models were not run, live APIs were not tested and independent accuracy comparison was not performed. Public ordinary-user APIs for OpenEvidence/AMBOSS were not confirmed.

Synthetic exercises covered unknown cold-product ingredients, duplicate paracetamol, SSRI/NSAID bleeding, codeine activation and failed time-separation assumptions. These are not clinical trials or personal cases.

## Curriculum and record design expansion

Belgrade/Sechenov programme coverage informed module 13. OpenStax was identified but not systematically studied. Modules 00 and 14 establish the strict exclusion of real records and general rules for any owner. FHIR principles inform provenance, prescription/actual-use separation and allergy uncertainty; the JSON templates do not implement FHIR.

The original general text/template audit checked privacy, valid JSON, agreement of CSV/JSON, 88 unique source IDs/URLs and valid source references. Templates preserve unknown medicine/allergy status. This is file/content control, not clinical validation.

## English distributable edition

The modules are translated and adapted for a general audience. Private-storage implementation is described as design guidance only, outside the public plugin. Sources retain explicit reading limits. Free integrations are optional research material. Build/evaluation results for this edition are recorded separately in `docs/validation.md`.

## 2026-10-08 expansion
