# Read the statistical results without changing the question

Checked 2026-10-18. These are selected within-study examples, not a ranked list of longevity treatments. RR denotes a relative risk/rate measure as stated by its source; HR compares event hazards. Neither is an individual's probability or a number of added years. Read the complete source-linked cards and JSON for population and model details.

| Question and population | Reported comparison and time | Estimate (95% CI) | Interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current cigarette smoking in a US adult cohort | Current versus never smoking; recruitment 1997-2018, mortality linkage through 2019 | Adjusted mortality rate ratio 2.80 (2.73-2.88) | Observational; not a randomized treatment effect. [medical:S767] |
| Quitting before age 35 in the same cohort | Former versus never smoking | Rate ratio 1.03 (0.99-1.07) | Near-null association is not identical personal risk; comparator is never smoking. [medical:S767] |
| Aerobic plus strengthening activity, US adults | Both recommended activity types versus insufficient levels; median 8.75 years | HR 0.60 (0.57-0.62) | Association; no guarantee of 40% fewer deaths for one person or 40% more life. [medical:S769] |
| Blood-pressure reduction in randomized trials | Standardized per 5-mmHg systolic reduction; no prior CVD; median 4.15 years | Major cardiovascular-event HR 0.91 (0.89-0.94) | Composite event, not ageing reversal; harms and universal targets not established here. [medical:S771] |
| Statin primary prevention, at-risk adults | Statin versus placebo/no statin; pooled varying trial horizons, 6 months-6 years | Mortality RR 0.92 (0.87-0.98); absolute difference -0.35 percentage points (-0.57 to -0.14) | About 3.5 fewer deaths per 1000 across those heterogeneous trial horizons; no one-year NNT. [medical:S772] |
| Metformin in a diabetes-risk trial cohort | Original metformin versus placebo assignment; median 21 years | Mortality HR 0.99 (0.79-1.25) | Mortality benefit not demonstrated; interval is not proof of equivalence or a reason to stop indicated treatment. [medical:S779] |
| Rapamycin added to exercise, 40 older adults | Rapamycin minus placebo; 13 weeks | Primary chair-stand difference -2.13 repetitions/30 seconds (-4.61 to 0.34) | Main ITT interval includes zero; sensitivity analyses are separate. [medical:S778] |
| CALERIE biomarker subset | Restriction versus usual intake; 24 months | DunedinPACE standardized effect d=-0.25 (-0.41 to -0.09) | Biomarker SD units, not years or observed mortality reduction. [medical:S677] |

For the mouse OSK experiment, 8.86 versus 18.5 weeks is remaining median survival after treatment at age 124 weeks. Adding the same starting age gives approximately 132.86 versus 142.5 weeks total median age, a derived difference of about 7.3%, rather than doubled whole life. This arithmetic illustrates denominator choice; it supplies neither an individual-data survival analysis nor a human conversion. [medical:S785]

The dataset additionally preserves adverse-event counts versus affected participants, within-arm IQR versus confidence interval, analysis-subset denominators, surrogate outcomes, and same-trial companion reports. Do not manufacture missing confidence intervals or convert a reported rate per person-time into cumulative risk.
