# Review nutrition with context

Reply in the user's language. Read [nutrition](references/NUTRITION.md) and relevant [condition support](references/CHRONIC.md) only as needed. A meal description is not permission to save a record. Use [health-response limits](references/19_SYMPTOM_REASONING_AND_SELF_CARE.md); suspected urgent illness follows health-first-aid when available.

Clarify the person's goal and only context that changes the advice: age/life stage, established conditions, medicines, food access/preferences, relevant allergies and a representative intake with quantities. Ask about restriction or distress when eating-disorder concerns are present; do not assume everybody wants weight loss. Children, pregnancy, frailty, kidney disease or complex clinical nutrition need their specific guidance and suitable professional support.

Distinguish estimated intake from measured consumption. State the food-composition source, portion assumptions and uncertainty in calories, protein, fat, carbohydrate, fibre and relevant micronutrients. Do not calculate a precise energy target from an incomplete diary or silently treat missing meals as zero. Explain energy balance and nutrient quality together; avoid moral labels, universal forbidden foods and unsupported supplement regimens.

For high lipids, glycemia or other condition questions, identify which result/diagnosis and clinical context are known. Explain source-supported food substitutions and dietary patterns, their limits and what medical risk assessment or follow-up may be needed. Do not declare diet a replacement for indicated medicines or infer a diagnosis from food habits. Preventive recommendations must be matched to life stage and jurisdiction.

Offer a small set of practical changes suited to the person's preferences and resources, with the reason, expected type of benefit and a review question. Show whole-pattern issues (adequacy, fibre, saturated fat, sodium, variety) where relevant without overinterpreting one meal. Distinguish food allergy from intolerance and avoid broad restrictive diets from unvalidated tests.

Review supplement evidence by ingredient, indication, deficiency evidence, dose/form and interactions through health-medicine-info when needed. Promising mechanisms, microbiome marketing and biomarkers do not establish patient benefit.

If the user requests recording, route the selected diary/summary to the authorized private archive. Do not export a real diary into general knowledge or web search. Offer text-based or available host question UI; never claim a specific interactive interface is universally installed.
