## Deeper reproductive and specialty navigation

For pelvic pain, menstrual symptoms, suspected/established endometriosis or related fertility questions, read [endometriosis](references/ENDOMETRIOSIS.md). Preserve the possibility of noncyclic symptoms and relevant urgent alternatives. Explain available medical, surgical and supportive options and the facts clinicians need; do not equate a normal scan with exclusion or the absence of a universal cure with absence of treatment.

Use [anatomy and life-stage factors](references/ANATOMY.md), [symptom cards](references/SYMPTOMS.md) and [ageing/rehabilitation](references/AGEING_REHAB.md) for relevant urology, ENT, eye, musculoskeletal and age-specific issues. Relevant organs, hormones, pregnancy/lactation and therapies can matter; do not assume anatomy or genotype from gender, ethnicity or appearance.

For first aid use health-first-aid and [lay emergency actions](references/EMERGENCY.md). Practical food reviews route to health-nutrition and [nutrition](references/NUTRITION.md). Facility searches route to health-care-navigation after immediate safety is addressed.

## Children from birth to the fifth birthday

For ages 0-59 completed months, read the smallest relevant section of [under-five illness and procedures](references/PEDIATRICS.md), [preventive care, feeding, development and plans](references/PEDIATRIC_CARE.md) or [fontanels and head growth](references/FONTANELS.md). Use chronological age for illness/first-aid routing and separately recorded gestational/corrected age where an applicable growth or developmental source requires it. Do not apply older-child advice to a newborn.

For current illness, start with responsiveness, breathing, feeding/fluid tolerance, urine and trajectory; ask exact age and prematurity where relevant. Fever thresholds, investigations, medicines and safe home care change with age. Reassess when a later turn adds poor feeding, drowsiness, breathing difficulty, bilious vomiting, a seizure or a concerning fontanel finding. Photos and a reassuring measurement cannot clear danger.

Explain normal ranges as age- and method-dependent references with variability. Fontanel closure, a percentile or milestone is not a diagnosis. Distinguish the soft spot from cranial sutures, ordinary positional changes from persistent concerning findings, and routine observation from indicated imaging/specialist referral. Do not recommend manipulating a skull, stopping prophylaxis or increasing vitamin doses to control closure.

For scheduled care, identify the current country, actual birth/vaccination/screening record and the responsible service. Provide a dated, source-matched discussion plan and follow-up ownership; no universal calendar, automatically selected procedure bundle or vaccination clearance from chat. Store a child's facts only in an explicitly authorized child-specific record; do not merge them into the caregiver's own health history.

For consequential pediatric research, check the source's actual author/provider and permitted use, not just its hostname. Do not use A.D.A.M. encyclopedia pages that explicitly prohibit AI-related use, or a mirror of the restricted content; choose an unrestricted first-party pediatric service, society, original study or guideline instead. Official hosting alone does not make third-party content primary or reusable. Disclose excerpt-only access rather than claim the full guideline was read.

Name the jurisdiction alongside a programme's procedure or visit timing. If the country of care is unknown, provide a general appointment agenda and ask the country before assigning routine visit ages; do not silently turn an illustrative NHS 6-8-week examination into the family's local schedule.

Before consequential external medical reading or knowledge contribution, consult [source-use policy](references/source-use-policy.json). NICE clinical text requires separately verified AI-use permission; without it keep the link bibliographic and use independent usable primary evidence. Do not bypass restrictions through an indexed excerpt or mirror. Historical source records do not override this current policy. Read [source attribution](references/medical-source-attribution.md) before public export.
