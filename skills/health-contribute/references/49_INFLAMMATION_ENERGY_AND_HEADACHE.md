## PATH-F3 — Injury, inflammatory response and infection are separate claims

A tissue response to injury is different from the injury or its cause. In the inspected toxicologic training, inflammatory-cell accumulation is interpreted alongside other tissue changes; an infiltrate can receive a different descriptive label. This illustrates the need for context, rather than a universal rule for human histopathology. The slide's rat/mouse terminology and required-feature convention must not be exported as human diagnostic criteria. [S713]

CRP is a blood marker of an inflammatory response, with several possible infectious and noninfectious contexts. It cannot locate or identify the cause by itself. Neither an inflammation label nor an isolated raised CRP establishes bacterial infection or benefit from a particular treatment; a normal result is not established here as an exclusion test. Do not interpret CRP as a calibrated inventory of whole-body inflammation. Source: MedlinePlus, National Library of Medicine. [S104]

## PATH-F4 — Regeneration and fibrotic repair describe tissue processes

Regeneration concerns replacement with the same tissue type; fibrotic repair adds connective tissue at an injured site. The inspected training connects these patterns with tissue framework integrity, but that pairing is a simplification, not a sufficient or exclusive predictor across organs. A tissue-process label does not measure recovered organ function or quantify impairment. No universal scar-strength, recovery-time or reversibility claim follows. [S713]

## BIO-F1 — Energy input, conversion and catalytic machinery do different work

ATP participates in cellular work, including signaling, contraction and synthesis, and is continually produced and used. Cellular respiration connects food-molecule processing with ATP production. This biochemical process is not a measurement of subjective energy. [S723]

A concrete institutional example concerns two linked artificial-cell populations: light-responsive machinery moves protons across a membrane; proton differences/signaling connect to ATP-producing enzyme machinery. Energy input, separation and conversion machinery contribute different parts of that system. This is an engineered research example, not a claim that light drives human-cell ATP production in that way. Source: U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Science, Basic Energy Sciences. [S724]

The existing catalysis card concerns activation barriers [S157]; this example concerns energy conversion. Neither concept alone demonstrates that adding an ingredient changes ATP in relevant human tissue or improves cognition/fatigue. Those require matching intervention and meaningful-outcome evidence. No universal supplement verdict is inferred.

## NEU-F1 — Headache pattern, causal category and imaging purpose differ

Describe onset, course and change, associated symptoms and context before treating an earlier label as an explanation of a new episode. Abrupt extreme pain or accompanying new weakness, seizure, confusion or visual loss calls for immediate emergency assessment in the inspected symptom-navigation source. Use the applicable local emergency service; a diary or long interview must not delay help. This is a selected warning-feature set, not an exhaustive triage tool. [S458]

Pregnancy/recent birth and a worsening or unusual attack change the assessment context. A prior migraine label cannot dismiss a changed presentation. The selected England care routes require local adaptation; this packet supplies no worldwide service number or complete pregnancy/child pathway. [S720; S458]

Primary headache disorders include migraine, tension-type and cluster headache. Secondary headache is attributed to another condition; medication-overuse headache is one example. These causal categories do not determine the cause of a particular episode or make a changed presentation safe. Source: World Health Organization, selected 2025 fact-sheet categories. No medication-use threshold or diagnostic criteria are supplied. [S722]

A CT scan provides internal images for a defined investigation or monitoring purpose, with professional interpretation and sometimes further testing. That general purpose does not select CT versus MRI for a headache or establish what a normal image excludes. [S721]
