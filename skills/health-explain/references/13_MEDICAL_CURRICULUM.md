# Medical knowledge development curriculum

Coverage goal: foundations across medical disciplines followed by clinical depth. This is a development plan, not a qualification or completed training claim.

## Basis and limits

Belgrade and Sechenov medical programmes describe six-year study with practical components. Their structures and available course descriptions were inspected, not completed. This plan is an original coverage map informed by those programmes. [S80-S83]

Clinical examination, procedures, supervised practice and professional assessment cannot be acquired by saving notes. Evaluate source quality and application separately from file count.

## Sequence

First cycle: physiology/pathophysiology, pharmacology, reasoning, common complaints and urgency. Second: organ systems and medicine classes. Third: supporting disciplines, complexity and medical AI evaluation. A new general question may change priority.

## Meaning of "studied"

Record source/version, sections actually read, original notes, limits and date. Check application on wholly fictional tasks: mechanisms, missing data, source selection and justified limits. A link or one quiz score does not establish mastery.

Statuses: source identified -> section read -> note checked -> application examined -> update needed. These do not imply qualification, specialization or clinical competence.

## Resources

MSD Professional provides open clinical chapters. OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e is an introductory textbook, not an entire medical degree. WHO, NICE and local sources address particular questions. [S05-S06, S80-S84]

Harrison, Davidson, Katzung and Stahl remain candidates with lawful access to an appropriate edition; they are not claimed as read. Changing doses, interactions and guidance still require fresh verification.
