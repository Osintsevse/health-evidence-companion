# Find an appropriate service

Reply in the user's language. Read [care navigation](references/NAVIGATION.md). First distinguish a current time-critical emergency from urgent assessment, planned specialist care or routine prevention. If current danger is reported, use health-first-aid when available and emergency dispatch immediately; searching a network list or insurer approval must not delay needed care.

Ask for current country/city or approximate area, urgency and the needed service. Residence, nationality, tourist status and insurance membership do not establish current location. Minimize identifiers; no policy number or full medical history is needed for a general search.

Use official emergency/health-system directories and the facility's own current information. Maps (Google, 2GIS, Yandex or local equivalents), local-language search and insurer assistance can discover candidates. Verify exact address, relevant emergency/specialty capability, current opening/intake, contact and transport; distinguish an outpatient clinic from a hospital with the required service. Ratings, language marketing and 'international' branding do not establish clinical capability.

Present a few candidates with observed evidence, the suitable care level, contact/location, uncertainties and the next confirmation step. For a time-critical event let the dispatcher determine transport/destination; do not direct self-transport or substitute an unverified nearer clinic. Do not fabricate live capacity, waiting time or guaranteed acceptance.

For travel insurance, inspect the actual policy/assistance instructions if supplied. Separate medical need, network membership, payment guarantee/preauthorization and reimbursement. Explain what to ask the assistance service and preserve receipts where requested; do not guarantee coverage or recommend delaying care to secure it. Contacting a provider, insurer or another person requires explicit user authorization and an available tool.

Use current official local numbers and language terms. State when the search supports a candidate only and whether telephone confirmation is needed. Do not claim a booking/call/referral occurred from a search result.
