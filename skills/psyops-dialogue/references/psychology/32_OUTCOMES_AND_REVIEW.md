# Reviewing benefit, deterioration and ending support
An original monitoring framework, 2026-10-08. Not a clinical scale or proof of treatment.

## Before practice
Agree on one functional goal and a short baseline observation. Ask what would be a useful change and at what cost it cannot be achieved. If only support is needed, do not require measurements. Don't collect a full history for the sake of a simple request.

## What to track
| Measurement | Observation example | What it doesn't prove |
|---|---|---|
| Life | Participation in a chosen action, work, conversation or rest | Diagnosis or remission |
| State | Brief self-rating | Medical assessment |
| Process | Time spent in rumination/checking | The only reason for the change |
| Harm/cost | Shame, avoidance, chat addiction, loss of sleep | Single event causation |
| Sports | Penalties, stability, clean comparable attempts | Psychological mechanism over time |

Distinguish between discomfort of voluntary practice and impairment of functioning/safety. Clarify the person’s own experience. Do not cancel his refusal by citing “resistance.”

## Clinical instruments
When requesting a scale, check the original form, age, language, version, period and terms of use; Do not reproduce items and scoring thresholds from memory. PHQ-9/GAD-7 are screening and outcome-monitoring tools, not a stand-alone diagnosis. A positive response to self-harm requires human clarification of the current situation; the overall score does not replace it. Do not use scales or low/medium/high ratings to predict suicide. [S52]

When comparing repeated results, consider time, changes in conditions, omissions, regression to the mean, concurrent care, and expectations. Do not calculate reliable change without the necessary normative data and a valid version of the tool.

## Decision after experience
It helped → keep the feasible version and check its stability. Unclear → clarify observation/conditions. Didn't help → reconsider the hypothesis and obstacle. Worsened → stop or change, assess the need for external assistance. Don't automatically increase intensity and promise that the next answer will fix everything.

## Completion
Name what the person understood, what they tried and what was actually useful; separate it from hypotheses. Form a plan if desired: early sign of difficulty, chosen action, available support, reason to contact a specialist. Completion does not require dependence on regular chat.

Records with real results are located only in authorized private storage. Empty templates/ forms can be passed; Completed ones cannot be published. Persistence and cross-chat memory depend on the actual capabilities and rights of the environment.
