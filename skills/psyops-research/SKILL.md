# Psychology evidence research

Package materials are in English. Respond in the user’s current language and follow an explicit language preference; never force English because the knowledge base is English.

Read [triage](references/psychology/30_EVIDENCE_TRIAGE.md), [research maintenance](references/psychology/10_RESEARCH_AND_MAINTENANCE.md) and [source register](references/psychology/sources.json); for new methods read references/psychology/29_MCT_AND_MODERN_EVIDENCE.md.

Specify population, problem, intervention, comparator, outcome and follow-up. Prefer applicable official guidance and original studies/reviews. Actually inspect the relevant text; record abstract-only access, search end date, publication date, correction/retraction status and access errors. Do not let a search snippet masquerade as full appraisal.

Separate within-group change from between-group effect, randomization from analytic samples, inactive from active comparators, symptom outcomes from performance/function and statistical significance from clinical meaning. Null significance is not equivalence. Check bias, missing data, adherence, conflicts and overlapping review samples. Do not automatically discard evidence solely because of funding.

Preserve positive results and limitations; distinguish insufficient evidence from evidence of no effect. Guidelines, theories, metaphors and original adaptations carry different kinds of support. Human therapy and other bots do not validate PsyOps.

Contribute original general prose with stable source IDs, scope, use and actual reading depth. Never copy a user's history, even unnamed, into examples or public issues. Update canonical knowledge, regenerate references and run package/privacy checks. External publication needs the user's instruction; an existing request to publish is authorization within that scope. Do not merge/approve others' proposed clinical claims as independently verified. Passing technical checks proves structure, not clinical truth.

For medical questions, cross-domain requests or selected private summaries, read [unified routing and record boundaries](references/35_UNIFIED_HEALTH_AND_PSYCHOLOGY.md). The combined installation does not authorize access to either private archive.
