# Sharing the general package
Version 2.0 · 2026-09-28

This package can be used as the basis of your own base: a review of sources, original instructions, exercises and blank templates. This is not a diploma, a clinical product, or a guarantee of safety.

Transfer only this shared folder or an individual shared skill. Personal profile, joint records, their addresses and archives are not included in the package. Do not share the parent folder if there are personal materials nearby. Research citations themselves do not provide rights to reprint articles or commercial workbooks.

The package was created as a new general edition. Examples should remain general or synthetic. Before the next transfer, check the contents and versions again: one accidentally added personal note breaks the separation.

The new user chooses the language, style, memory consent and his own goals. The previous user's preferences are not inherited. The general rules against sycophancy, pressure and fictitious diagnoses remain.

Do not claim certification, professional association affiliation, or clinical validation of this package. Third-party sources and full protected texts are not included here. Public posting and changing permissions are not automatic.
