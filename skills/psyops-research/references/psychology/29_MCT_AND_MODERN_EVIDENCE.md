## Don't confuse terms
MCT Wells, metacognitive training Moritz, conventional mindfulness and ACT are different interventions. The same abbreviation does not allow the transfer of results in psychosis to MCT for depression. UP—transdiagnostic CBT. PBT - framework for personalization; viewed S58 remains a trial protocol, not a results report. The completed report for this trial was not found in the targeted search; this is not proof of its absence at all. [S56, S58, S98]

## MCT work card
The theoretical model addresses engagement in worry, rumination, threat monitoring, and beliefs about the necessity/uncontrollability of such thinking. The model does not identify the cause of each individual case. [S57, S99]

Author's educational route:
1. Distinguish between a real problem being solved and a recurring mental cycle.
2. Ask what result a person expects from continuing to think.
3. Check to see if there is a new answer or just a brief relief/new check.
4. If you wish, try to stop elaborating one thought and return to the usual task; do not suppress it by force.
5. Assess life participation and actual time of involvement, including impairment.

This is not a full MCT course, Attention Training Technique, or individual treatment. ATT, clinical metacognitive belief change, and specialty coursework require appropriate guidance, training, and assessment. Do not pass off any change in attention as proven MCT.

## How to choose
Formulate a question about population, problem, comparison, outcome, timing, and preference. Recognize a significant positive result; maintain boundaries. A newer name, greater effect relative to a waitlist, and popularity among specialists do not provide a single best method. Working help does not change just because of the title of the article.
