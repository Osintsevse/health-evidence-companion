# Private psychological notes

Package materials are in English. Respond in the user’s current language and follow an explicit language preference; never force English because the knowledge base is English.

Read [boundaries](references/psychology/20_DATA_BOUNDARIES.md) and [review](references/psychology/32_OUTCOMES_AND_REVIEW.md). Blank templates are examples of format, not permission to record.

Ground the actual owner and destination before writing. Inspect permissions, including inherited access and sharing by link; distinguish separate accounts belonging to the same person from unrelated recipients. Use available authorized host tools, never invent access. Read current content before an update and preserve originals, provenance and concurrent changes.

Explicit owner instructions and existing authorization determine what may be saved. Retain the minimum useful summary; label reported experience and hypotheses separately. Respect corrections and "do not save". Full transcripts, third-party records or new recipients require the corresponding scope. An individual agreement does not authorize a joint journal.

Store actual notes outside the public checkout, including ignored directories and generated archives. Keep IDs, private links, filled forms, screenshots, telemetry and operational logs out of GitHub, public source registries and general search queries. General learning must be reformulated as an independent question and checked using public sources.

Verify writes by read-back and state precisely what was saved. Do not promise guaranteed confidentiality, complete deletion from service backups, autonomous monitoring or automatic access across chats. The storage service and model host have their own access/retention policies. This skill is instructions, not a confidential clinical backend.

For medical questions, cross-domain requests or selected private summaries, read [unified routing and record boundaries](references/35_UNIFIED_HEALTH_AND_PSYCHOLOGY.md). The combined installation does not authorize access to either private archive.

For a requested cross-domain reader or clinician export, read [selected-summary sharing](references/private-record-sharing.md). Reuse the [local summary helper](scripts/psychology_records.py) with already owner-selected text, an explicit owner key and private paths. It does not load or convert a full diary. A projection config is not proof of consent; omit it for medical-only exports.
