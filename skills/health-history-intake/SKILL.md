# Focused history and a useful review

Reply in the user's language. Read [history and assessment](references/DIAGNOSTICS.md), [condition support](references/CHRONIC.md) as relevant, and [response limits](references/19_SYMPTOM_REASONING_AND_SELF_CARE.md). Address reported current danger before a questionnaire or archive operation.

Clarify whether the user wants an explanation, visit preparation, a focused symptom interview or a broader voluntary history. Offer questions through the host's available question UI or ordinary chat. Ask a few decision-relevant questions at a time, allow 'unknown', 'prefer not to answer' and free text, and adapt to the answer. Do not force the same long interview for a simple request or repeat already resolved facts.

For a present concern, establish onset/trajectory, associated symptoms, impact on function, relevant exposures/conditions, actual medicine use, allergies/adverse reactions and what has already been assessed. For a broader history, organize conditions and operations, family reports, medicines/supplements, vaccination/screening evidence, reproductive/life-stage context where relevant and practical goals. Distinguish event date, report date and current status. Unknown information is not a negative history.

Use the minimal authorized accepted archive when the user refers to it and access is available. Preserve provenance, contradictions and stale information; an old prescription is not current use and a family condition is not the owner's diagnosis. Do not read an entire psychological diary to complete medical history.

When a photo is useful for a nonurgent appearance/label/report description, request only appropriate, non-identifying content, preserve uncertainty and explain limits. Do not request an intimate image, diagnose from one image or delay emergency action. A clinical score requires its verified tool, intended task, complete inputs, population and version; explain missing applicability rather than inventing a risk number.

Produce a dated review with selected coverage, priority problems, observed facts/dates, interpretation and uncertainty, useful questions, source-supported next step and reassessment trigger. Separate a report flag, urgent present concern and historical abnormality. Give balanced findings: neither false reassurance nor unsupported catastrophizing. Check who follows up a pending result when relevant.

Explanation alone does not authorize storage. For requested updates, use health-record-import and the exact chosen private destination. Link every proposed fact to its source and review status; keep interpretations separate from accepted clinician diagnoses. A source-linked AI review is not clinician validation, a referral or a prescription.

Use the [optional question bank](references/intake-question-bank.json) as prompts, selecting only relevant questions. It contains no answers and is neither a mandatory checklist nor a scored medical instrument.
