# Broad health learning map

Reviewed 2026-10-17. This is a continuing curriculum and coverage map. Priority topics change study order; they do not exclude other organ systems, everyday questions, life stages or psychological knowledge. Inclusion is not a claim of completed reading or clinically validated answers.

## Keep three layers connected

1. **Foundations:** anatomy/physiology, pathology/immunology, pharmacology, epidemiology/statistics/measurement, psychological constructs and research methods. Preserve exact source edition and sections. A mechanism explains a possibility; clinical benefit needs applicable outcome evidence.
2. **Clinical and everyday questions:** symptoms, tests, treatment evidence, prevention, function and rehabilitation across organ systems. Include common concerns and consequential alternatives. Match country, age and exact product.
3. **Life stages and circumstances:** newborn/child development, reproductive health/pregnancy, adulthood, older age/frailty; disability, work, sport, travel and access. Evidence does not automatically transfer across groups.

The foundations index, clinical index and specialty/life-stage routing remain the wider curriculum. This map makes uneven coverage and new questions explicit.

## Study and contribution loop

Each study batch should include a foundational question, a currently relevant clinical question and a less-covered family. This is a selection rule, not scheduled background work or a quota overriding urgency. Keep cardiovascular, pulmonary, renal/urological, endocrine, infectious, GI, neurological, musculoskeletal, skin, eye/ENT, reproductive, nutrition and mental-health coverage open through the specialty curriculum.

For each question preserve population/setting; observations/mechanism; alternatives; diagnostic meaning/test limits; outcome-specific benefits/harms; source edition and exact section/page; actual reading date/scope; uncertainty and recheck trigger; skill and response-check status. Store a readable original explanation and source-linked study record.

Bibliography, indexing, section reading, reviewed note, bundled reference and observed model response are distinct states. A book or specialty does not become learned from indexing or inclusion here. Publish concise original general knowledge and evidence metadata. Protected instruments/tables and exact clinical regimens require separate applicable evidence/rights review. Keep unexecuted checks and substantive maintainer review visibly pending.
