# Medical source attribution and reuse boundaries

Checked 2026-10-09. Repository-written explanations remain original synthesis; linked works keep their own rights. The repository MIT licence does not relicense external medical publications, charts, images, tools, provider terms or data. No protected full texts, tables, questionnaires or datasets are supplied.

NHS main-site information is used under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), subject to exclusions and [NHS terms](https://www.nhs.uk/our-policies/terms-and-conditions/). Contains NHS information adapted from [www.nhs.uk](https://www.nhs.uk/). Adapted factual explanations are original, may omit detail and have source-specific check dates; this is not NHS endorsement. Third-party stock imagery and other credited material are excluded. England/UKHSA government programme sources retain their stated Crown copyright/OGL notices, local scope and version dates.

The limited crying/fontanel factual summary attributed to NSW DCJ contains information from State of New South Wales (Department of Communities and Justice), used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) according to the [DCJ copyright notice](https://dcj.nsw.gov.au/statements/copyright-and-disclaimer.html). Accessed 2026-10-09; original summary, not source wording. No PDF, image, logo, personal photo or endorsement is included. Third-party attribution would require reassessment.

Other clinical sources are linked and briefly summarized as original facts with specific access/rights recorded in source-review.json. No blanket open licence or AI permission is assumed. The source-use policy (`source-use-policy.json`) controls providers with explicit restrictions; historical bibliography is not permission. NICE is retained as a human-verification pointer until separately matching AI-use permission is established. Its clinical content is not used as active evidence in the new under-five routes or fixtures. Older source records are retained; this extension does not claim a complete independent rights audit of the legacy corpus.

On2026-10-17, Cochrane.org terms clauses5.2–5.4 were inspected. The site explicitly restricts automated analysis and AI development/validation; its historical records are permission/bibliographic pointers only until matching permission is verified. Detailed claims solely dependent on those records were removed from the respiratory, intervention-watchlist and general-health-check notes. This source-use limit is not an efficacy finding; independently authored evidence needs its own appraisal.

NEJM Group terms, January2026, sections2 and4 explicitly address AI processing/analysis/output. No matching permission was verified. The identified S384/S426 and psychology:S41 work/linked summaries remain bibliographic context only; dependent claims/routes/fixtures were corrected. This is a source-use decision, not an efficacy verdict or a completed audit of every historical provider. WHO documents retain their CC BY-NC-SA3.0IGO conditions and third-party exclusions; the new notes contain brief original factual synthesis, not adapted tables/tools.
