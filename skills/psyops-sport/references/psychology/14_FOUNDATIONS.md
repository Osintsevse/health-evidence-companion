## Disciplines and therapeutic schools are not one thing
| Field | What questions does it help to ask? | Error transferring to chat |
|---|---|---|
| Cognitive psychology | How do attention, memory, and interpretation influence experience? | Consider a memory as a video recording or any unpleasant thought as a distortion |
| Psychology of learning | What reinforces action and avoidance? | Explain a complex life with one reason |
| Social psychology | How do norms, roles, and situations influence behavior? | Draw a causal inference about a specific person from an average effect |
| Developmental psychology | How do challenges and opportunities change throughout life? | Attribute everything to childhood without data |
| Personality psychology | What relatively stable differences exist between people? | Replace the description with a typological verdict |
| Clinical Psychology | When does suffering and disruption require assessment and assistance? | Make a diagnosis based on similarity to the description |
| Health Psychology | How do mental, physical and social factors interact? | Call a symptom psychosomatic before medical examination |
| Family and systems approaches | How do people's actions influence each other? | Equalize responsibility in cases of violence |
| Existential tradition | How to deal with choice, meaning, freedom and finitude? | Propose one correct worldview |

It is a guide for asking questions, not a textbook of disciplines, nor a complete assessment of every scientific theory.

## Multi-level explanation
Check several levels: current situation; sleep/stress/health; actions and habits; thoughts and attention; relationship; history; social constraints. Do not declare any level as the root cause without data.

For example, sexual difficulty may require interaction talk and medical evaluation. These explanations are not mutually exclusive. [S28]

## Function and form of behavior
The same action can have different functions: silence can be rest, confusion, avoidance, punishment or a way to maintain safety. You cannot infer function from form. Ask about context and consequences.

Understanding the function helps you choose the next step, but it does not justify causing harm.

## Emotion as information, not proof
Fear communicates the threat experienced, but does not itself establish its likelihood. Anger may indicate a violated expectation or boundary; the specific explanation is being tested. The absence of a strong emotion does not prove a lack of love or importance.

Do not pit emotions against rationality: evaluate the accuracy of statements and the appropriateness of actions, leaving room for feelings.

## Memory and hypotheses
Do not suggest forgotten traumas, do not perform “memory restoration” and do not present the emerging image as evidence of an event. The statement “you are suppressing” cannot be made an unfalsifiable explanation for any disagreement.

When faced with conflicting memories, hold onto uncertainty and work with what is known now. Don’t choose the winner of an argument based solely on the narrator’s confidence.

## Causality
- After the event does not mean because of the event.
- The simultaneous change of two phenomena does not establish the direction of the cause.
- A group result does not guarantee an individual result.
- The questionnaire indicator does not equal the diagnosis.
- The name of the mechanism is not a test of it.
- A therapeutic metaphor need not describe neurobiology literally.

## Alliance
Research shows the relationship between working alliance and therapy outcome. This is not an argument for pleasing: a shared goal, an agreed way of working, and the ability to correct misunderstandings are more important than automatic approval. Causality cannot be established by correlation. [S36]

WHO EQUIP views competencies as subject to training and observable assessment. For AI, this is a useful idea of behavioral quality; self-testing of the model does not replace external professional assessment. [S18]

## What to study next for a specific need
Differential diagnosis, trauma, eating disorders, addictions, complex personality conditions, neuropsychology, psychopharmacology, family violence, legal responsibilities, and work with minors require separate specialized reviews. This database is aimed at the adult user and his stated topics.
