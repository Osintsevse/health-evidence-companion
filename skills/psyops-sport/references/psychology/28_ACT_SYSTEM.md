# ACT: Flexibility, Context and Action
Educational map 2026-10-08; author's questions are not a validated protocol.

ACT seeks to expand the ability to act on chosen values in contact with the present. All six processes work together rather than as required six steps in every conversation. [S14]

| Process | Author's question for self-exploration | Protection against misuse |
|---|---|---|
| Acceptance | Is there room for experience while I choose to act? | Do not tolerate violence or malfunction |
| Defusion | Do I notice the thought or am I obligated to obey it? | Don't declare facts as thoughts only |
| Present moment | What significant signals are available now? | Don't narrow your focus when driving |
| Self-as-context | Is it possible to notice experience without condemning the entire person? | Do not promise a special state of consciousness |
| Values | What quality of my action is important to me? | Don't force family, success or forgiveness |
| Committed action | What feasible step does this direction express? | Don't require a heroic load |

## Functional choice
Clarify what problem the habitual struggle with inner experience solves and what price it has. Relief in itself is not bad. If a strategy supports the person’s life, there is no obligation to abandon it for the sake of “proper ACT.” If trying to achieve complete certainty takes time and action, suggest briefly noting the thought and returning to the chosen task.

Value is the direction of behavior, goal is an achievable result. It’s possible to value caring and set boundaries, to value skill and to decline an unsafe start. Accepting the internal experience does not determine whether to remain in the external situation.

## Practice and revision
First agree on the format; then select one process that is actually related to the task. A short verbal practice is preferable to a complex meditation if one is overwhelmed. If detachment, panic, or loss of orientation increases, stop and return to normal external support; in case of persistent deterioration, discuss specialized assistance.

Evaluate participation in chosen life, behavioral flexibility, and cost of practice. Reducing discomfort is possible, but not a mandatory criterion. If a practice has become a mandatory ritual before action, change it. Don't use "you don't accept" as an accusation for lack of progress.

## Evidence and boundaries
A review of meta-analyses supports a range of uses of ACT but does not establish overall superiority over CBT. Evidence from professionally delivered psychotherapy does not support the effectiveness of a specific implementation through PsyOps. [S12] Process theoretical language is useful for organizing issues; the proposed mechanism, the protocol effect, and the brief exercise effect are different statements.
