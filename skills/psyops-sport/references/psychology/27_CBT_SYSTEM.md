# CBT: a coherent system instead of individual techniques
Author's educational map, 2026-10-08. Supports discussion and safe self-help, does not present chat as a course of treatment.

## Core questions
NIMH describes psychotherapy goals that include symptoms, daily functioning and quality of life, and emphasizes treatment fit and practitioner training. Ask what problem is being addressed, why the approach fits, what the goals are and how progress will be reviewed. This project organizes formulation, optional practice and review into an educational map; that organization is not itself a validated clinical protocol. Selected Beck practice concepts are documented separately in packet45. [S110, S105]

## What to differentiate
| element | Work question | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Automatic evaluation | What testable conclusion emerged here? | An unpleasant thought may be accurate |
| Rule/Assumption | What does a person require of himself or consider obligatory? | Do not derive a rule from one word |
| Persistent belief | Is the expectation repeated in different situations? | Don't label your personality |
| Avoidance | What does it protect you from now, what does it deprive you of later? | Sometimes leaving protects safety |
| Safety behaviors | Which protective behavior prevents the person from testing their prediction? | Do not cancel genuine safety measures |
| Reinforcements | How do consequences make an action more likely? | Don't blame for a quick fix |
| Rumination | Is thinking creating a new answer or going in circles? | Not all thinking is pathological |

This chart is a working distinction, not a diagnostic tool.

## Behavioral experiment
Pre-write down the forecast, degree of confidence, feasible action and observed outcome. Choose a situation without real medical, road-safety, financial or interpersonal risk. After the experience, compare expectations and data; consider other explanations. Success is a clarification of the prediction, and not necessarily the disappearance of anxiety.

A completely synthetic example: a fictional adult expects that a small clarifying question to a colleague will always cause irritation. He selects a common work question and observes the answer. You cannot turn the experience into a test of your partner’s love, a provocation of conflict, or a dangerous ride. Such examples are not adapted from personal stories.

## Activation and problem solving
NIMH includes coping and problem-solving among possible psychotherapy elements. For optional everyday reflection, choose one feasible action, identify its purpose and obstacles, and review what happened. This is an original planning aid, not a course of behavioral activation or a treatment claim. Lack of resources is not corrected by a positive thought. Do not prescribe exercise for an unknown condition. [S110]

## Exposure and ERP
NIMH describes compulsions as including mental actions, and ERP as a form of CBT involving a safe supported approach to triggers while resisting the usual compulsive response. Initial anxiety can contribute to dropping out; assessment and appropriate professional support matter. This educational description does not supply an individual exposure hierarchy or ritual/neutralization protocol. [S111]

Treatment fit depends on the particular problem and person. Do not turn an explanation into self-prescribed high-risk exposure, trauma processing, interoceptive exercises or sleep restriction. For insomnia, use the separately studied sleep packet and its actual source limits. This assistant boundary does not substitute for condition-specific evidence. [S110; medical:S657]

## Work between conversations
The assignment is voluntary and commensurate with capabilities. Clarify the meaning, the obstacle and the way to notice the result. If not met, investigate the reasons and simplify rather than declare insufficient motivation. Don't demand a diary as the price of getting help.

## Revision and completion
Compare the functional goal, subjective state, frequency of the desired action, harm and observation conditions. If there is no benefit, check the initial hypothesis, consistency of practice, real barriers and need for other assistance. Do not automatically intensify assignments. Finally, identify early signs of relapse, useful actions, support and reasons to contact a specialist. Detailed template - 32.

This map increases the coherence of knowledge, but does not imply the completeness of all CBT protocols or the qualifications of the implementer.
