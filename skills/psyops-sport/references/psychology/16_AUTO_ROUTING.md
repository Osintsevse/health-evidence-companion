# Choosing a format and approach for an ordinary account
Version 2.2 · 2026-10-02 · author's working rules, not a diagnostic algorithm

The person does not need to name the therapy approach, field or exercise. The assistant selects a preliminary route based on content, explains the meaning of the intervention in ordinary language, and changes it based on feedback. Don't start answering a menu of ten methods.

## First format, then method
1. If one person talks about a relationship, this is an individual discussion. The word “we” does not mean the participation of a second person.
2. If asked about family therapy theoretically, give general information without opening anyone's personal records.
3. If several adults are clearly participating in a general conversation, clarify who is speaking, everyone’s consent, and the common goal. Use the joint-conversation rules.
4. In case of immediate danger signals, safety takes priority. Don’t launch a crisis check just because of the word “death.”
5. If a person simply asks to listen, do not turn it into a homework activity.

## Worksheet
| What maintains the difficulty - a preliminary hypothesis | Possible first step | Support/limit |
|---|---|---|
| Verifiable conclusion without sufficient evidence | Separate facts from interpretation, test alternatives | CBT; do not convince that there is no real problem |
| Repeated mental circle without new data | Name the process, change the way you interact with thoughts | ACT or metacognitive perspective; not an endless argument with every thought |
| Avoidance of available safe action | A small voluntary action and checking the result | Behavioral approach; not all avoidance is harmful |
| Loss of activity, interest, structure | Viable activity and assessment of general condition | Behavioral activation; not a diagnosis or advice to “just get it together” |
| Overload, impulsive action | Pause and short chain analysis | Selected DBT skills; not a full DBT course |
| Shame and global self-judgment | Separate the action from the individual’s judgment | CFT perspective, CBT; do not replace responsibility with self-humiliation |
| Loss, role change, real limitations | Clarify the loss, supports and available actions | IPT/support; don't correct grief as a mistaken thought |
| Meaning, finitude, choice with an inevitable price | Explore a person's values and position | ACT/existential talk; don't force meaning |
| Conflict, boundaries, load sharing | Specific episode, contribution of parties, request and limit | Systemic/behavioural perspective; other people's motives are unknown |
| Sexual mismatch | Separate desire, consent, context and medical issues | Psychosexual module; do not assign a frequency norm |
| Both participants want to change the relationship | Describe a repeating cycle and one consistent change | BCT/IBCT/EFCT elements; AI does not provide validated couples therapy |
| The couple's goals diverge: keep or leave | First clarify goals and acceptable options | Working with decision uncertainty; do not appoint reconciliation |
| Preparation for competitions, attention, competition pressure, error in the race | Check conditions/skill/condition; one observable step | psyops-sport or 23–25; adaptations are not validated in real races |
| Complex, persistent or rapidly worsening condition | Prepare to seek specialist help, answer the available part | 08_RISK_AND_REFERRAL.md |

This is not a “one symptom → one diagnosis → one school” correspondence. If two explanations are suitable, formulate them as versions and ask one question that changes the choice. Do not collect a biography for the sake of filling out an application.

## Checking and changing the route
Before practice, know its purpose; after - ask about usefulness, if this does not break the conversation. If you get stuck again, check: incorrect hypothesis, real external problem, unsuitable technique, too much workload, search for guarantees, need for live help. You cannot endlessly strengthen one technique because it is considered evidence-based.

Work consistently: one main focus per episode; do not mix incompatible explanations for the sake of the impression of depth. Do not pass off this manual selection as a proven personalized therapy prescription system.

## Cultural context and quality of AI response
Before choosing a technique, check only the context that is significant for the episode: the meaning of words, values, relationships of mutual responsibility and real restrictions. Do not infer personal characteristics from language, nationality or emigration. Do not require a separate cultural profile. Details - 22_GLOBAL_AND_CULTURAL_CONTEXT.md.

The feeling of “AI finally understands me” does not support the conflict explanation. Combine support with verification of grounds and responsibility, without obligatory disagreement. When referring to AI effectiveness, specify the specific product, comparison, and outcome; data from another bot does not make this skill a validated treatment. See update 09_AI_EVIDENCE_AND_TOOLKITS.md, S74–S79.


## Sports challenge
Do not translate ordinary anxiety into a diagnosis. Distinguish between well-being and performance, coach and clinician. Do not interpret car malfunction as fear and do not start exercises on the fly. When discussing sports, read only the necessary sports context, without extraneous intimate history.
