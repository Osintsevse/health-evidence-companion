# General knowledge policy

Applies to this repository, its releases, archives, examples and retrieval corpora.

## Allowed

General medical explanations, guideline summaries, medicine mechanisms and interaction information, source metadata, educational curricula, general workflows and entirely blank templates. Educational cases must be wholly fictional and explicitly marked as synthetic; never adapt a real person's history into a case.

## Excluded

Information about an actual individual: symptoms, diagnoses, test results, actual medication use, treatment dates, psychiatric or psychological histories, document photographs, conversations and personal summaries. This applies to users, relatives, partners and other people. Keep these only in a separate, authorized private archive.

Deleting a name is insufficient. Do not commit pseudonymized real cases, personal identifiers, private file links, filled templates, screenshots of records, API keys or logs of individual queries. Do not include these in a general search index or AI training corpus.

## Learning from a personal question

Formulate an independent general question; verify it against published sources; save only the general explanation, scope, limitations and references. Do not copy the original message, prescription, measurements or sequence of personal events. Record an actual episode separately only within an authorized private system.

## Before saving or exporting

1. Classify each item as general knowledge, blank template or private record.
2. Inspect text, filenames, metadata, links and archive membership.
3. Store a filled template outside this repository; its link also remains private.
4. Do not infer access to records from access to the knowledge base. Sharing private information requires a separate decision by its owner.
5. If private information is committed accidentally, stop publication and correct all affected copies. Deleting the current file does not remove Git history, releases, caches or existing downloads. Follow `SECURITY.md`; do not repeat the sensitive content in a public issue.

Module 14 describes record design. The public plugin explains those rules and blank fields; it does not operate a patient archive or collect protected health information.
