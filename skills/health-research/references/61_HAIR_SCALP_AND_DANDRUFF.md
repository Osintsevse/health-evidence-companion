# Hair shedding, scalp changes and dandruff

## What changes the assessment of hair loss?

Ask when it began, whether the change is widespread or patchy, whether hairs are coming out or breaking, and whether the scalp is painful, inflamed, scaly or crusted. Ask about recent illness, childbirth, weight change, new medicines and tight hairstyles. Widespread shedding can become noticeable months after a major stressor. A delay can fit the history, but does not identify the cause. A thinning part or a receding hairline can coexist with shedding. Bring the timeline and medicine or supplement list to an assessment. [medical:S512, medical:S513, medical:S849]

Repeated tension on the hairline can cause breakage and hair loss. A painful hairstyle can be loosened or removed immediately. Persistent traction can lead to permanent loss. Prompt dermatology review matters with rapidly expanding patches, soreness, inflammation, or a shiny hairless area. Scaly patches with broken hairs may need evaluation for infection. A shampoo or vitamin trial should not postpone that evaluation. [medical:S850, medical:S651, medical:S512]

## What to do about flakes

For typical mild dandruff, regular gentle washing may be sufficient. If using an anti-dandruff shampoo, apply it to the **scalp** and follow the local product's contact-time and rinsing instructions. Hair texture, scalp oiliness, dryness and irritation can affect how often washing is tolerated. A curly or coily hair shaft can dry out even when the scalp needs treatment. Severe or persistent scale, pain or patchy hair loss deserves an examination for conditions such as seborrheic dermatitis, psoriasis or tinea. Infant cradle cap has its own care pathway. [medical:S703, medical:S651]

**A Serbian label example.** The ALIMS summary for Hemofarm Mycoseb 20 mg/g ketoconazole shampoo, revised August 2020, specifies dandruff and scalp seborrheic dermatitis. Its directions cover adults and adolescents over 12 and say to consult a doctor or pharmacist if the desired effect has not occurred in four weeks. Use the current leaflet for the pack in hand. This historical label does not establish present-day pharmacy stock or the approved indication of another country's ketoconazole product. See module 46 for the named US labels. [medical:S853, medical:S705, medical:S706]

**What an eight-week trial measured.** In the STRATUM trial of moderate to severe seborrheic dermatitis, 304 people aged 9 or older were assigned to roflumilast foam and 153 to vehicle foam. At week 8, 79.5% versus 58.0% had clear or nearly clear skin with at least a two-grade improvement on the investigator scale. The US label reports an adjusted difference of 20.6 percentage points (95% CI 11.2 to 30.0). This trial provides no comparison with ketoconazole shampoo, no estimate of relapse after discontinuation and no basis for using prescription foam for ordinary flaking. The US label includes a contraindication for moderate to severe liver impairment. [medical:S854]

## Biotin and supplements marketed for hair

Treating a confirmed deficiency and taking extra biotin for unexplained thinning are different clinical questions. NIH's fact sheet found little evidence supporting general hair-growth claims. FDA reports falsely low troponin results with some susceptible assays. Before testing, tell the clinician or laboratory the supplement name, amount and last dose. The relevant test and assay determine appropriate instructions. Urgent assessment for chest symptoms should proceed promptly. [medical:S851, medical:S852]
