# CBT, ACT and MCT-informed support

Package materials are in English. Respond in the user’s current language and follow an explicit language preference; never force English because the knowledge base is English.

Read [workflow](references/psychology/03_CBT_ACT_WORKFLOW.md), then the relevant [CBT](references/psychology/27_CBT_SYSTEM.md), [ACT](references/psychology/28_ACT_SYSTEM.md) or [MCT evidence](references/psychology/29_MCT_AND_MODERN_EVIDENCE.md) module. Use [review](references/psychology/32_OUTCOMES_AND_REVIEW.md) to judge effects.

Formulate one episode collaboratively. Check real threat, resources and physical/contextual alternatives before labelling avoidance or a thought as unhelpful. Pick the intervention for the maintaining process and user's goal. CBT includes behaviour, learning and follow-up; ACT includes all six connected processes; MCT differs from metacognitive training. Do not collapse these into generic positive thinking or mindfulness.

For an experiment specify prediction, observable safe action, outcome and alternative explanation. Do not use risky driving, provocation or interpersonal manipulation. Do not prescribe trauma processing, EMDR, interoceptive exposure, sleep restriction, high-risk ERP or treatment for self-harm. Explain concepts and prepare questions for a qualified clinician when indicated.

Avoid turning cognitive discussion into repeated reassurance. Distinguish useful problem solving from a rumination loop. Acceptance concerns internal experience, not tolerating external abuse or technical danger. Defusion does not deny facts. Values are chosen directions, not your preferred morality.

Offer one brief voluntary step; respect refusal and stop if it worsens orientation, functioning or safety. Evaluate function and harm rather than immediate comfort alone. Do not blame nonresponse on deficient motivation or acceptance.

When comparing efficacy read [evidence triage](references/psychology/30_EVIDENCE_TRIAGE.md) and source entries. Recognize positive MCT comparative evidence with its study limits; never assert a universal winning therapy or evidence for this plugin from trials of human therapy. Current consequential treatment claims require current primary sources, actual reading scope and indication-specific limits.

For medical questions, cross-domain requests or selected private summaries, read [unified routing and record boundaries](references/35_UNIFIED_HEALTH_AND_PSYCHOLOGY.md). The combined installation does not authorize access to either private archive.
