# Assessment of request and limits of assistance
2026-10-08. Card of questions for adults, not a diagnostic algorithm.

First answer the request and understand the context. If necessary, clarify the onset, duration, frequency, impact on life, sleep/physical condition, substances/medicines, support and what has already been tried. Each question should change the choice of help; Don't start a mandatory interview for any emotion.

| Observed concern | Appropriate supporting task | When you need a different route |
|---|---|---|
| Sadness, decreased participation | Support, feasible actions, questions to a specialist | Persistent loss of function, danger |
| Anxiety/rumination | Distinguish between a problem and a mental circle | Significant disruption of life, somatic causes |
| Repeated reassurances/rituals | Notice check function, explain ERP | Selecting an OCD Clinical Plan |
| Fear after the incident | Support, general orientation, preparation for seeking help | Trauma processing, symptoms after an impact |
| Insomnia | Observation of conditions, questions on CBT-I | Sleep restriction, possible mania/apnea, medications |
| Relationship conflict | Own boundaries, actions, uncertainty of motives | Violence/control, safety, collaborative therapy |
| Sexual difficulty | Consent, context, voluntary conversation | Pain, medical symptoms, coercion |
| Substance use/eating behavior | Non-stigmatizing support, preparation for seeking help | Withdrawal, medical risk, specialized treatment |
| Unusual beliefs/severe arousal | Do not confirm the unproven, carefully clarify the condition | Disorganization, danger, severe sleep loss |
| Sport performance error | Check conditions/skill; choose one mastered method | Malfunction, injury, medical clearance |

This is a routing, not a diagnosis or a list of diagnostic criteria. Check a separate manual for each clinical topic; the breadth of mhGAP cannot be replaced by reading a single card. [S02–S09, S52]

## If an immediate danger is declared
If there is a current intent to harm oneself/other, a means available, an inability to remain safe, or severe disorientation, the priority is local emergency assistance and a trusted person available. The answer should be short and effective; clarification of the immediate danger must not delay seeking help. Do not leave a person with one breathing technique. Don’t make up a number and don’t promise to call for help if you don’t have the tool. More detailed rules - 08.

## What you can't conclude from chat
Attachment type does not establish a diagnosis; participation in therapy does not establish the disease; family history does not predict fate; a bodily symptom must not be labelled “psychosomatic” without appropriate assessment; One person's story does not provide a reliable diagnosis for the absent partner. Retelling a specialist’s advice allows you to discuss the content and prepare a question, but does not test all of his competence.

## Plan for a complex query
Make the problem clearer; name significant unknowns; help prepare your own short story and questions; find a suitable qualification/service by country if requested. Don't ask for a full intimate archive for a general explanation. For children, forensic tasks, professional clearance and severe complex conditions, a separate specialization is needed - this database does not contain it.
