# Support and consulting in motorsport
Author's training and consulting design program, 2026-10-08; not a professional qualification and not a validated course for improving racing performance.

## Two goals and several professions
Athletic performance and well-being should be assessed separately. A fast driver may need clinical assistance; an anxious driver can safely perform a mastered task. Psychological training, instructor work, engineering assessment and medical clearance require different competencies. AASP describes CMPC education, subject areas, mentoring, and credentialing; This is a human certification, not an automatic qualification as a clinical psychologist, nor is it a universal license from any country. [S94, S97]

## Agreement with the driver and team
Determine who the client is and who pays; goals and limits of the service; who sees sports observations, psychological records and medical information; what can be communicated to the team. Payment by a coach does not authorize access to the driver’s personal diary. Agree on a specific amount of feedback. Do not transmit information covertly, do not build coalitions. Professional Guidelines - AASP Code of Ethics. [S93]

## Single-episode assessment
Clarify the discipline, experience and format of attempts. Compare training and competition: track, traffic, tires, weather, damage, preparation, sleep, illness, measurement errors. Psychological explanation is one hypothesis. Check that the action has already been mastered: a beginner's mistake is not automatically choking. Telemetry shows performance, but does not read minds.

| Stage | Work while stationary | Sign for revision |

| Development | Try it in familiar training | Adds distraction/ritual |
| Transfer | One pressure factor in organized settings | Technical risk is growing |
| Competition | Only a familiar short cue | A new technique introduced on competition day |
| Check | Performance, well-being, safety, results separately | Improvement attributed without comparison |

After stopping, one reason, an alternative and the next check are enough. During movement, long conversations, closing the eyes, breathing tasks, recordings, telemetry exercises and work with trauma are not carried out.

## What is known and unknown
A sports meta-analysis shows that positive average estimates are sensitive to design quality and objective outcome selection [S80]. The F1/F2 study uses a small sample and 2019 data: it does not show that a specific skill produces a champion [S87]. You cannot convert the size of the effect into lap seconds or promise a transfer from simulator → track → auto slalom/drift. Well-being and comfort are not a substitute for objective performance.

## After an incident and during overload
If there is a possible concussion, contact the medical team; chat does not determine diagnosis or return. The FIA emphasizes recognizing and reporting symptoms. [S96] Do not suppress protective fear in the event of a malfunction, do not train courage by increasing speed on the road. Return after injury is discussed with a medical specialist, organizer and instructor; clinical fear/traumatic symptoms - with a mental health professional.

Sleep, travel, nutrition and organizational stress are part of the conditions, and not proof of psychological weakness. Do not prescribe sedatives, stimulants or sleep deprivation. End of the season/career, identity beyond results, relationships with the team and selection pressure are on the agenda, but serious deterioration requires separate help.

## Preparing a person for a profession
Study program: basic psychology and research methods; evaluation and formulation; ethics/confidentiality; motor learning and attention; sports interventions; clinical boundaries; understanding of a specific discipline; supervised practice; observable skill tests. Check the exact requirements for education, title and practice in the selected country. This package is a training guide and not the end of such training.
