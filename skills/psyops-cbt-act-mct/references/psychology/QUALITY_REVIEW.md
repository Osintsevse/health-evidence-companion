All combinations of context, stability during long conversations, effectiveness for a specific person and real work with a couple have not been tested. Instruction separation does not provide technical isolation of the model's memory.

Correct real mistakes based on feedback; repeat affected scenarios after significant changes. Do not use personal cases in public tests.
