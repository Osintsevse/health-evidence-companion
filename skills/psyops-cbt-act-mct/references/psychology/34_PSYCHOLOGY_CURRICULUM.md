# Map for further study of psychology
2026-10-08. A learning route, not a diploma or a complete textbook.

## Scientific foundation
Research question, operationalization, experiment and observation; sampling and transferability; measurement reliability/validity; causality; statistical uncertainty; publication bias; reproducibility and research ethics. Be able to explain why the group’s result is not a prediction for one person. Do not replace research with a “neuro” explanation.

## Basic disciplines
| Field | What to learn to distinguish | Practical test of understanding |
|---|---|---|
| Learning | Consequences, avoidance, skill acquisition | Different functions of the same behavior |
| Cognitive | Attention, memory, evaluation, decision | Observation versus reconstruction |
| Social | Norms, role, situation, relationships | Alternatives to the personality explanation |
| Development | Changing challenges and opportunities in life | Rejecting an obligatory childhood explanation |
| Personality | Description of differences and measurement | Trait versus diagnosis and typological label |
| Health | Physical, mental, environment | Medical alternative to the psychological hypothesis |
| Clinical | Distress, functional impairment and indications for care | Boundary of support and treatment |
| Cultural | Language, norms, conditions, preferences | Individual clarification without stereotype |
| Sports | Performance, learning, well-being | Comparability of conditions and individual outcomes |

The table sets learning objectives and does not claim completion of a review of all theories. Basic distinctions - 14; culture - 22; sports - 23–25 and 31.

## Practical foundation
Contact and agreement → joint formulation → choice of method → feasible action → verification of benefit/harm → review → completion. A psychologist also needs supervised practice, supervision, legal responsibilities, and appropriate education. AI cannot be assessed by a diploma issued by its own instructions.

## What does this version cover?
Adult conversation about experiences, life choices, relationships, consent, meaning, sports training; educational cards for CBT, ACT, MCT and alternatives; source register with actual reading depth; limited planning for safe self-care; private records with authorization.

## What remains a separate work
Complete disorder-specific manuals and supervised delivery; neuropsychological testing; child and adolescent psychological care; eating disorders and addictions as treatment courses; complex trauma/personality-related conditions; psychopharmacology; legal/judicial tasks; medical or racing clearance; independent assessment of competencies; PsyOps clinical research.

For a new gap, formulate a specific general question and check primary sources. Do not declare “full psychology” after adding a large number of files. Develop a database based on indications and observed errors, without copying personal stories into a public corpus.
