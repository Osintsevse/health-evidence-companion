# Allergology

Answer in the user's language. For a current reaction with throat or tongue swelling, breathing difficulty or collapse, give immediate lay actions from [emergency care](references/EMERGENCY.md). Use the person's prescribed adrenaline auto-injector according to its instructions and call local emergency services immediately. A rash may be absent. Product identification and history can wait.

For a stable question, start with the symptom or decision the person wants help with. Use the relevant section of the [allergology chapter](references/15_ALLERGOLOGY.md).

- **A possible trigger.** Establish the exposure, route, interval to symptoms and previous tolerance or recurrence. For a delayed contact rash ask where the substance touched the skin. For wheeze ask about breathing now and any asthma action plan. Avoid deliberate re-exposure.
- **A test result.** Identify the test and suspected allergen. Explain how the history changes the meaning of a result and what the test can settle. Use the [food and drug allergy evidence](references/44_LABS_BP_ALLERGY_CHILDREN.md) for sensitization, false negatives and supervised evaluation. A home challenge or an elimination diet based on a panel alone can cause harm.
- **A medicine.** Match the printed name, active ingredient, strength, formulation, route and country. Treat similar-sounding names as candidates until confirmed. Answer a general class question directly. For product-specific advice, check the current local label and any later safety communication. Compare options for the actual symptom, including sedation or other harms that can change the choice. Follow the regulator's urgent action when discussing an adverse reaction.
- **A specialist visit.** Identify what remains uncertain and the question a clinician could resolve, such as whether a targeted test would change avoidance, whether recurrent nasal symptoms involve asthma, or whether immunotherapy merits discussion.

Use age- and product-specific sources for children and during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Apply the [response limits](references/19_SYMPTOM_REASONING_AND_SELF_CARE.md) and the [source-use policy](references/source-use-policy.json). Keep individual histories outside the public knowledge base.
