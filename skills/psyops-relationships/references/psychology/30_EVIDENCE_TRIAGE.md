# Evidence: what is confirmed, what requires verification
2026-10-08. Editorial classification, not GRADE's own formal rating.

## Assertion levels
| Status | What can I say | What cannot be concluded |
|---|---|---|
| Guideline recommendation | In a specified population, a specific protocol is proposed | Suitable for everyone or fully implemented here |
| Comparative study | Under these conditions, a difference in this outcome was obtained | One method is best for any task |
| Preliminary/mixed data | There is a signal, restrictions require clarification | Promise clinical benefit |
| Theory/metaphor | Helps organize questions | Cause established or neurobiology confirmed |
| Author's adaptation | Has a reasonable rationale and verifiable results | Validated by the same RCTs as the original therapy |
| Not appraised | This knowledge base has not established a conclusion | Method proven or disproved |

## Found weaknesses
Many source records only have access to an abstract, indexed excerpt, or bibliographic record. The mere presence of 102 source records does not mean 102 full-text examinations and independent tests. The 2026 audit retains the original dates and separately adds a log of the actual re-audit.

24 T cards, 8 M cards, paired check-in, route selection and new educational cards - original designs. Their individual effectiveness has not been established. Confirmation of psychotherapy, a sport technique or other bot does not confirm our package. Historical response checks do not constitute clinical validation.

MCT is now evaluated taking into account the newer synthesis [S98–S99]. PBT does not receive proven replacement status for CBT from protocol [S58]. IFS/CFT are assessed on a specific program/outcome basis, with no universal promise [S60–S61]. Emotionally Focused Couples Therapy and tapping EFT are not interchangeable. [S24]

## Watchlist without false final verdict
NLP, family constellations, hypnotherapy, somatic experiencing, gestalt and individual branded explanations require their own indication, comparative studies and risk assessment. They are not combined into one category. A 2012 review of NLP reported a small body of weak evidence for health outcomes; the current full-text re-evaluation has not been completed. [S102] NLP is not offered here as a proven treatment. This cannot be a conclusion about all conversations or all techniques called NLP.

Do not offer repressed memory retrieval as a fact finding, a “reading” of personality type motives, an attachment diagnosis, deterministic stages of grief, or a universal neurobiological history for a particular person. These are rules of cautious inference, not a complete overview of all theories.

## Checking a new publication
Record PICO question, design, sample, outcomes, and timing; separate active control from a waitlist, randomized enrollment from analytic samples, and within-group changes from comparisons. Check missing data, registration, primary outcomes, corrections, conflicts, and transferability. Do not use statistical non-significance as evidence of equivalence. Do not stack overlapping reviews as independent confirmations.

Distinguish between “insufficient evidence,” “evidence of small/no effect,” and “recommendation against.” An access error does not prove either effectiveness or lack thereof. To recommend from limited reading, clearly indicate what could not be assessed. New entries are included in sources.json; sources from public blogs provide only the direction of the search, not the final clinical conclusion.
