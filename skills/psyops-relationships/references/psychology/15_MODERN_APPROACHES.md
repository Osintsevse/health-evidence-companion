# Modern Approaches: Is There Anything Better Than CBT and ACT?
Version 2.0 · verified 2026-09-28

Update 2026-10-08: MCT evaluation expanded with new comparative meta-analysis and critical reading of the 2026 review. The former table reflects the original review; current rating and restrictions are [29_MCT_AND_MODERN_EVIDENCE.md](29_MCT_AND_MODERN_EVIDENCE.md). MCT is no longer described by RCT alone. [S98–S99]

## Short answer
In the sources reviewed, there is no basis for declaring one method to be the best for all people and tasks. The novelty of the approach, the attractiveness of the explanation, and the superiority over the waiting list do not prove superiority to a good alternative therapy. CBT is an evolving family of protocols; ACT isn't the only modern option either.

“Better” needs to be revealed: fewer symptoms, fewer relapses, better relationships, higher quality of life, less time/cost, fewer unwanted effects - in which group and over what period of time? For personal meaning, the question does not necessarily boil down to a clinical scale.

## What's added to the main map
| Direction | Idea | What was actually compared? | Practical solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| MCT, metacognitive therapy | Dealing with how a person engages in worry and rumination | In an RCT of 174 adults with depression, MCT scored better on the BDI-II but not on the HDRS; only two therapists, possible researcher allegiance [S57] | Consider as an alternative for a specific task; do not make an overall rating based on one study |
| Unified Protocol | General emotional processes rather than separate guidelines for each diagnosis | In an RCT of 223 adults with anxiety disorders, the authors found equivalence to disorder-specific protocols and lower attrition [S56] | This is transdiagnostic CBT, not a refutation of CBT; relevant when difficulties overlap |
| Process-based therapy | Individual formulation of processes and selection of interventions | There are small studies; found clinical material comparing with conventional CBT - trial protocol, not a results report [S58–S59] | Use the idea of verifiable personalization; Do not call free mixing of techniques full-fledged PBT |
| Compassion-focused therapy | Reducing destructive self-criticism and shame, developing a caring attitude | A 2025 review collected mixed studies rather than proving universal superiority over CBT [S60] | Suitable for consideration in shame; goodwill does not require agreement with erroneous conclusions |
| IFS | The language of inner "parts" and conflicting urges | In a small RCT PARTS for PTSD, both groups improved, but there was no significant difference with active controls [S61] | Don't declare it useless, but don't promise superiority either. “Parts” - a model of experience, not found objective entities |

## Already included alternatives
DBT, MBT, schema therapy, IPT, behavioral activation, MBCT, brief psychodynamic therapy, trauma-focused techniques, and EMDR have different indications and limitations. The validity of one protocol for a specific disorder does not make it a universal tool for discussing relationships. Details and primary links - 01_EVIDENCE_MAP.md.

For couples, data on couple interventions is needed: BCT/IBCT and EFCT are considered separately. For prevention - separate studies of brief paired checks on the state of relationships. It is not that these formats compete with individual CBT on one general scale. [S24–S26, S63–S65]

## How not to choose according to fashion
1. Formulate a useful result and a specific difficulty.
2. Compare reasonable options based on indications, preferences, past experience and price.
3. Distinguish between a full clinical protocol and a short self-help element.
4. Check whether the chosen method helps in real life. If there is no benefit, reconsider the hypothesis rather than blaming the person.
5. Don't change a working approach just because of a new name; don't hold on to it just because of past benefits.

The review is targeted, not exhaustive. It does not establish the optimal method for a person without discussing their situation, and does not validate the effectiveness of implementing any method through this AI.
