# Couple preventive conversation
Version 2.0 · own template, not validated by Marriage Checkup

The goal is to notice changes and speak up before conflict builds up. This is an optional meeting of two consenting adults, and not an examination of a good partner. Not suitable for a situation where the participant is afraid of the consequences of speaking up.

## Short meeting
A guideline of 15–25 minutes is a convenient option, not a therapeutic dosage.
1. Each person names one specific moment of contact that was pleasant or useful. It's fair to say that it's hard to remember now; gratitude is not required.
2. Everyone chooses one area of stress: everyday life, workload, money, intimacy, sex, rest, plans. Not all at once.
3. One describes the episode and the request; the other first checks whether he understood correctly, then answers. Swap roles.
4. If possible, agree on one small experiment: who does what, when, how we notice the effect, how to refuse or change the agreement.
5. Finish on time. Move a difficult question to an agreed upon point or to a meeting with a specialist.

## Options for neutral questions
- “Where are you most stressed right now?”
- “When did you find it easier or harder this week to be with me?”
- “What do you want to keep and what do you want to change?”
- “Is there a topic that we keep putting off?”
- “What is everyone ready for, and what is not yet ready?”

## What not to do
Do not use the meeting to calculate debts, evaluate a partner, force sex, or demand an account of private messages. Frequency of sex should not be a mandatory homework assignment. It is possible to agree on a time for contact, but consent to specific actions remains free at each moment.

The indicator of benefit is more clarity and feasible agreements, and not necessarily a good mood after the meeting. If conversations consistently worsen the situation, reconsider the format rather than increase their frequency.

Research from professional Marriage Checkup provides grounds for studying preventative formats, but does not confirm the effectiveness of this particular template or its delivery by AI. [S64]
