# Finding a doctor in Russia or Serbia

Checked 2026-11-25. Use this guide to arrange a routine appointment, check a clinician's professional details or ask how a visit will be paid for. Establish the person's **current city**, the specialty or service, adult or child care, and the intended payer. A city or district is usually enough. Enter account credentials and insurance identifiers only on the official service when needed for the person's own transaction.

For a suspected life-threatening emergency in **Russia**, call **103** for an ambulance or **112** for emergency services. In **Serbia**, call **194** for an ambulance or **112** for emergency services. Give the dispatcher the current location. A provider search, coverage inquiry or language check can wait. S290 S500

## Russia

1. **Find a provider under compulsory medical insurance (OMS).** Look up the current regional compulsory-insurance fund (TFOMS) and its register of participating medical organizations. Match the region, provider, address and service. Ask the insurer named on the policy about the covered pathway if the register is unclear. A participating clinic can also sell visits privately. Confirm which route the appointment uses before agreeing to pay. [Regional fund guide](https://lofoms.spb.ru/files/2024_vopros_n6.pdf). S835
2. **Make a routine appointment.** Check which polyclinic you are attached to (prikreplenie), then use the clinic's registry, its regional booking service or the Gosuslugi doctor-booking service. The Toksovo hospital instructions for Leningrad Oblast describe selecting a branch and a slot in the region of attachment. Ask whether this specialty needs a referral (napravlenie). In Moscow, the city polyclinic FAQ lists a GP and certain specialists for direct booking. These local lists and digital routes vary. If the list or schedule is empty, ask the clinic's registry about available appointments and referral requirements. [Gosuslugi booking](https://www.gosuslugi.ru/600204/1/form) · [Leningrad Oblast hospital instructions](https://www.trbzdrav.ru/patients/services/gosuslugi/making-an-appointment-with-a-doctor.php) · [Moscow FAQ](https://pt.mos.ru/faq/). S836 S837
3. **Check the clinic.** Get the legal name, tax identifier (INN) or licence number from the clinic's official website. Search the Roszdravnadzor register. Compare the licence holder, status, site of practice and the service you need against the proposed visit. A trading name can cover several legal entities or branches. The regulator also explains how to obtain an extract when the search result is unclear. [Licence search](https://roszdravnadzor.gov.ru/spec/services/licenses) · [Regulator's instructions](https://www.roszdravnadzor.gov.ru/medactivities/licensing/faq/244). S838 S839
4. **Check the doctor.** Use the clinic's official staff page. Ministry Order 118n lists the professional details to publish, including name, position, education, specialty and validity period of accreditation or specialist certificate, and hours. Check that the specialty and branch match the service. Ask the clinic to clarify inconsistent or missing details. The federal accreditation centre's Gosuslugi extract instructions describe an authenticated request for the professional's own record. They do not provide a public lookup of any doctor by surname. [Order 118n, Appendix 1, item 6](https://dgp140.mos.ru/global_ruffe_tech/docs/118nmz2025.pdf). S840 S841
5. **Private insurance, self-pay or a visit from abroad.** For voluntary insurance (DMS), have the insurer confirm the particular provider, branch, specialty and any authorization required. For self-pay, ask the clinic for the consultation price and separately charged tests or procedures. For foreign nationals, Government Resolution 631 distinguishes free emergency care for a life-threatening condition from planned care, where compulsory coverage if eligible, voluntary insurance, a paid agreement or an applicable international agreement can matter. Confirm eligibility for a planned visit with the payer. [Resolution 631, rules 3–8](https://dgp140.mos.ru/global_ruffe_tech/docs/631prf2025.pdf). S840 S842

## Serbia

1. **Find a doctor in the public insurance pathway.** For someone covered by RFZO, ask the local *dom zdravlja* (primary health centre) which *izabrani lekar* (chosen doctor) accepts new patients. Ask for general medicine or the appropriate child or women's service. Serbia's Health Insurance Act describes a declaration of choice and a chosen doctor's role in referring patients for specialist assessment (*uput*). Confirm with the centre how to register and where any required referral should go. The centre's roster and local process can change. [Health Insurance Act, articles 139–143, including Gazette 109/2025](https://www.vrh.sud.rs/sites/default/files/attachments/%D0%97%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%20%D0%BE%20%D0%B7%D0%B4%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%BC%20%D0%BE%D1%81%D0%B8%D0%B3%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%9A%D1%83%20109-25.pdf). S843
2. **Book a visit.** The eZdravlje help page explains how to view slots and book the chosen doctor under *Zdravstvo* then *Zakazivanje termina*. If the portal shows an old doctor, no doctor or an unusable slot, contact the *dom zdravlja*. Its local data feed may be delayed. In Novi Sad, for example, the health centre publishes its own contact centre for bookings and questions about services. Look up the equivalent centre in the patient's municipality. [eZdravlje help](https://portal.e-zdravlje.gov.rs/help) · [Dom zdravlja Novi Sad](https://dzns.rs/zakazivanje-kontakt-centar/). S844 S845
3. **Check a physician's licence.** The Serbian Medical Chamber (Lekarska komora Srbije, LKS) publishes a dated list, *Evidencija lekara sa važećom licencom*. Open the surname group and match the spelling and licence number. The inspected list has fields for specialty, subspecialty and validity dates. Use the date printed on the list. If a name is ambiguous or a record appears missing or stale, ask the clinic for the exact spelling and licence number, then seek clarification from the Chamber. A listed specialty alone says nothing about experience with a particular procedure. [Chamber register](https://www.lks.org.rs/clanovi-lks). S846
4. **Check the establishment separately.** Use the Serbian Business Registers Agency (APR) and choose *Jedinstvena evidencija subjekata u zdravstvu* when a practice may be registered as a private entrepreneur. This combined search covers healthcare institutions and private practices. Match the legal entity and location to the provider's own branch information. Confirm service, clinician, age group, appointment, language and price directly with the branch. Registration alone cannot establish any of those details or RFZO coverage. [APR healthcare searches](https://www.apr.gov.rs/registri/zdravstvene-ustanove/pretraga-podataka.4394.html). S847
5. **Visitors and private cover.** For planned care ask the home insurer and RFZO what an applicable agreement covers and which documents or providers it requires. Serbia's foreign ministry explains the role of the health-insurance branch for the place of temporary stay when the agreement provides a certificate route. Check the agreement for the country of insurance and the exact service. For commercial insurance, obtain the insurer's instructions for that branch and service, including direct billing, authorization and reimbursement. For self-pay, request the price and what it includes before the appointment. [Foreign ministry guidance](https://www.msp.gov.rs/gradjani/usluge/sporazumi-o-socijalnom-osiguranju). S848

## A practical confirmation

In Russia, ask whether the doctor accepts OMS, whether a referral is required and at which address the appointment takes place. In Serbia, ask “Da li je za ovaj pregled potreban uput? Da li je pregled preko RFZO ili se plaća? Na kojoj adresi lekar prima?” Separately confirm any interpreter or accessibility needs. Report what the official source establishes and what the branch or payer still needs to confirm. No personal accounts, bookings, benefit determinations or individual licence checks were used to prepare this guide.
