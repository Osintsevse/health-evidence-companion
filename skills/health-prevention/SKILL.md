# Prevention and informed decisions

Reply in the user's language. Read [policy](references/00_KNOWLEDGE_POLICY.md), [routing](references/26_CLINICAL_ROUTING_AND_SCOPE.md) and [prevention and shared decisions](references/29_PREVENTION_NUTRITION_AND_SHARED_DECISIONS.md). Include the established short informational disclaimer. A symptomatic question belongs to clinical assessment even when asked as a check-up request; address stated emergency signs first.

For a personalized checklist, establish only decision-relevant age, country, relevant organs/operations, pregnancy where relevant, risk factors, prior findings and vaccination evidence. Retrieve current applicable local guidance. Distinguish screening, diagnostic investigation, monitoring and surveillance. Do not turn US age thresholds into Serbian/Russian schedules.

Present eligible options with the reason, interval/version, expected benefit, false positives, overdiagnosis, burdens and follow-up. Mark what depends on missing information. Do not recommend annual laboratory packages, imaging or tumor markers merely to be thorough. “Insufficient evidence” is not the same as proven ineffectiveness.

For vaccines read [vaccination](references/17_VACCINATION.md); for nutrition/activity/longevity use module 29 and the [evidence gate](references/18_TREATMENT_EVIDENCE_AND_HOMEOPATHY.md). Separate patient-relevant outcomes from biomarkers and animal evidence. Do not prescribe an anti-ageing medicine/supplement regimen or a restrictive diet from weak evidence.

Explain numerical benefits and harms using [risk communication](references/32_RISK_COMMUNICATION.md), with consistent denominators and time horizons. Prepare a concise visit agenda and questions that support the person's preferences. For insurance, inspect the actual scheme/contract and document only truthful clinician-supported indications. Do not invent eligibility or promise reimbursement; obtaining approval is separate from medical appropriateness.

For children, pregnancy or postpartum questions, use available health-family-care and [life-stage guidance](references/28_SPECIALTY_AND_LIFE_STAGE_ROUTING.md). Persistent private plans follow [incremental archive rules](references/24_INCREMENTAL_ARCHIVE_SAVES.md) only when requested. No automatic archive, insurer transmission or periodic monitoring is created by using this skill.

## Source-grounded inference

For consequential prevention explanations and contributions, read [source-grounded reasoning](references/39_GROUNDED_REASONING.md). Match each claim to its actual source, reading scope and population; distinguish measurements and biomarkers from clinical outcomes, and state applicability limits and uncertainty.

## Nutrition, chronic risk and ageing

For a requested food review, read [nutrition](references/NUTRITION.md) and use health-nutrition when available. Explain source-supported food patterns and substitutions for the actual clinical goal; avoid a precise calorie deficit or supplement regimen from incomplete information.

Use [chronic conditions](references/CHRONIC.md), [ageing and function](references/AGEING_REHAB.md), [anatomy/life-stage factors](references/ANATOMY.md) and [diagnostic-tool applicability](references/DIAGNOSTICS.md) for risk-dependent prevention. Family history, genetic ancestry and social/environmental factors are distinct. A racial label is not a genotype, a risk calculator requires its validated population/inputs/version, and historical genetic reports do not independently prescribe screening or treatment.

For newer therapies and prevention claims, separate demonstrated clinical benefit, approved indications and jurisdictions, availability, investigational options and unsupported marketing. Give balanced alternatives and the next decision or follow-up, not a global health verdict.

For care from birth to the fifth birthday, use [pediatric preventive care and plans](references/PEDIATRIC_CARE.md). Match examination, newborn screening, growth/development, vaccination and dental/vision recommendations to exact age, country, birth risks and completed records. Explain what a procedure can answer and who follows up the result; no universal screening or catch-up bundle.

## Find packaged knowledge

Use [the knowledge search guide](references/knowledge-search.md) when you need to locate a relevant packaged note or trace its sources. On a host with Python, the bundled `scripts/knowledge_search.py` provides bounded local search and section reading; otherwise use the linked reference indexes. Search only the general packaged material, preserve reading and source-policy limits, and inspect the selected section before using it. A search hit is a navigation aid, not a new appraisal or a clinical conclusion.

## Longevity evidence

For lifespan, healthy ageing, geroprotectors, smoking cessation or future rejuvenation, read [the longevity entry](references/57_LONGEVITY_AND_PREVENTION.md), then only the relevant complete card in `references/longevity/`. Use the [statistics guide](references/longevity/40_STATISTICS_GUIDE.md) for numerical comparisons. Preserve source reading scope, outcome, denominator, comparator, time and uncertainty. Distinguish observational associations, randomized clinical endpoints, biomarkers, animal results and sponsor-only reports. Do not invent personal breakthrough probabilities or prescribe an experimental longevity regimen. Recheck rapidly changing trial evidence before consequential use.
