# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

Health Evidence Companion welcomes respectful participation regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, appearance, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, education or experience. Participants should make it safe to ask questions, acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, use clear language and respect different communication needs.
- Critique claims and contributions with evidence rather than attacking people.
- Accept constructive feedback, acknowledge errors and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Discuss general medical and psychological knowledge without pressuring anyone to disclose personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats, insults, sustained disruption, unwanted contact and retaliation against reporters are unacceptable. Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission is unacceptable.

## Privacy in this project

Never post actual medical or psychological records, personal histories, filled forms, prescriptions, document photographs, credentials or private links in issues, pull requests, discussions or examples. Removing a name is insufficient. Use wholly fictional examples explicitly labelled synthetic. See [contribution rules](CONTRIBUTING.md), [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) and [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Scope and responsibility

These rules apply in project-managed GitHub spaces and when someone officially represents the project. The maintainer, [@Osintsevse](https://github.com/Osintsevse), is responsible for moderation and may remove or edit inappropriate contributions, lock discussions or restrict participation. Enforcement should consider severity, impact and repeated behaviour.

## Reporting concerns

Follow the reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for exposed private information, credentials or security concerns. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting only if it is enabled and the concern fits that channel.

For other conduct concerns, use GitHub's platform abuse-reporting controls where applicable. If a private maintainer channel is needed, open an issue asking only for a private contact route; do not include allegations, names, screenshots, sensitive details or disclosure locations. This project does not currently advertise a verified private email or a dedicated confidential conduct-reporting channel. Wait for an agreed private route before providing sensitive material.

The maintainer should minimize disclosure, avoid repeating sensitive content publicly and handle reports fairly. Absolute confidentiality and a response deadline are not promised. A reporter may request reconsideration through the same agreed private route; a sole maintainer does not provide an independent appeals body.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and context, the maintainer may request a correction, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or impose a permanent ban. Serious threats, harassment or privacy violations may require immediate restrictions. Retaliation or repeated violations may escalate the response.

This is an original project policy, not an adoption of a third-party code-of-conduct template.
