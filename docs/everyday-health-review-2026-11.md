# Everyday health evidence review, November 2026

The November expansion addresses questions people ask before choosing food, changing sleep or exercise habits, or arranging a medical visit. The [knowledge index](../knowledge/00_INDEX.md) links the chapters. Each chapter identifies the population, jurisdiction when relevant, and which sources were actually read.

## Corrections to earlier material

- The everyday sleep note used to list work on apnea and insomnia as pending. Modules 42 and 43 already contained selected evidence. The revised note directs readers to the relevant work and the newer material on shift schedules.
- The Serbian emergency-number example previously asked the responder to verify the number before recommending one. In a medical emergency the corrected entry leads with 194. Routine checks of facilities, payment and referral follow a separate pathway.
- The nutrition table mentioned kidney disease and heart failure in its discussion of potassium salt substitutes. WHO's conditional adult recommendation has additional population limits. The new text addresses pregnancy, children, impaired potassium excretion, and shared meals.
- The knowledge index gave an outdated count of medical skills. It now points to the skill catalogue.

## Bibliographic audit

On 25 November, 49 medical source records in the starting register had a direct PubMed or PMC article locator that could be resolved to a PMID. We compared their recorded titles with NLM summary metadata, including linked correction, concern and retraction relationships. Five indexed correction notices were attached to four studies. These links are maintenance signals. They do not show by themselves that a number, a treatment conclusion or an entire study has changed. This check excluded sources with a publisher URL alone and did not systematically search other databases. Earlier clinical review dates remain distinct from the metadata lookup.

The two corrections for the Hall inpatient nutrition trial leave its daily energy-intake contrast intact. One revised details of the report and supplement, the other revised meal-specific estimates. For SPIRIT, a comparison of the paper versions identified a corrected table cross-reference. For the blood pressure meta-analysis, the correction concerned the contributor list. Reading records S336, S376 and S771 document the scope.
