## Findings that change interpretation

- Sampling variability belongs to an estimator under a specified process. Standard error is not individual-observation spread or known realized error. Normal approximation of proportions does not make binary outcomes normal. Larger samples do not remove bias or design dependence.
- CHAP preserves a favorable primary estimate whose interval includes no effect, separately from a favorable secondary mortality signal. Randomization was by community; the intervention had multiple components and some continued. No individual drug, device or isolated screening benefit follows. A narrative event-rate denominator inconsistency was noticed; that rate is not exported.
- The sport paper's abstract overstates instructional benefit relative to its discussion's nonsignificant difference, and a discussion sentence reverses the noise/error direction reported in results. No established superiority to silent control or general athlete harm is inferred. Proposed anxiety/motivation mechanisms were not directly tested.
- The independently read arthritis overview also lists therapies without a comparative appraisal. That list is not imported as an efficacy endorsement. The older obesity page's drug table, broad numerical stopping rule and2024 approval list are not exported as current universal guidance.
