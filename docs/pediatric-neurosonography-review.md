## Evidence review

- Use AWMF S2k 006-102 v2.0 for age-dependent presentation, the purpose and limits of imaging, the distinction between anatomy and pressure, and clinical follow-up. Canadian neurosurgical recommendations distinguish initial assessment from imaging in established hydrocephalus. They describe their own clinical setting.
- Fandak et al. studied reproducible reading of particular acquired views in a predominantly young term-born sample. The study did not establish repeat-acquisition reliability or a minimum meaningful serial change. Beijst et al. and Valverde et al. identify separate measurement and protocol contexts for preterm infants with hemorrhage.
- Krahulik et al. support the limit of negative fundus findings in a selected neurosurgical population. Cour-Andlauer et al. address an indirect ultrasound proxy in pediatric intensive care. Rohde et al. report associations in children already selected for imaging. These populations and outcomes delimit the inference.
- The updated treatment review uses AWMF and CNS/AANS for management choices and scope. The CNS/AANS recommendations against acetazolamide plus furosemide and routine serial lumbar punctures apply to specified goals in preterm posthemorrhagic hydrocephalus. Kennedy's author abstract documents the one-year trial outcomes. The small retrospective cohort for idiopathic intracranial hypertension documents specialist use and cannot establish pediatric acetazolamide efficacy.
- Vinchon's treated historical cohort and Zahl's external hydrocephalus follow-up address different conditions or treatment histories. Both support discussing function and follow-up over time. Neither supplies an individual forecast for an isolated ventricular finding.
- Two holder-posted Russian product documents and sponsor-submitted trial fields illustrate how formulation, age, trial exclusions and assessed outcomes limit a proposed use. The live regulator records were not independently verified. The FDA explanation supplies useful general questions about prescribing rationale while its regulatory framework applies to the United States.
- Public guidelines and ordinary copyrighted material are cited with original factual synthesis. Open articles retain their article-specific Creative Commons attributions. The review does not redistribute figures, datasets, tables or proprietary algorithms.
