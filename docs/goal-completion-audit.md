## Broader source and claim work still required

Review the named curricula against actual source scopes, rather than adding a numerical pass for every heading. Assess anatomy/physiology, pathology/immunology, pharmacology and measurement foundations; cardiovascular, pulmonary, renal/urological, endocrine/metabolic, GI/hepatic, infectious, neurological, musculoskeletal, skin, eye/ENT, reproductive, nutrition and mental-health coverage; newborn/child, pregnancy/postpartum, adult and older/frail applicability. Retain useful existing modules while distinguishing selected source study, overview, pointer-only and unresolved evidence.

For each consequential gap, record its impact, current safe informational scope, usable next source/section and the evidence needed to close it. A gap is not resolved by a catalogue entry, search snippet, source count, green CI or the absence of an observed failure. Completion must be established requirement by requirement; this document currently records incomplete work.
